Interpretation and limitations:
Adaptive MiniMax responds more accurately and is more stable than disabled mode; raw decision flips alone are not evidence of correct rule use.
PR/PQ performance is stronger than PC/PS, but HOL type is confounded with domain and multi-component decision-vector difficulty.
PC conditional denominators are only 8, 5, and 4; avoid treating 75% GLM PC sensitivity as robust superiority.
CRS conditions on model-specific original correctness. A common-correct subset is included for the MiniMax comparison.
The selected subset consists of formally verified eligible perturbations with canonical fact presentations, not the full benchmark distribution.
Results provide behavioral evidence of sensitivity to decisive facts under fixed rules, not proof of faithful internal rule reasoning or proof of shortcuts.
GLM mixes providers; provider defaults/quantization may differ. MiniMax adaptive is not forced thinking.
No GPU is needed for exact-answer counterfactual metrics.
